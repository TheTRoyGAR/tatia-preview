The designer

Tatiana Hoffmann

TATIA™ is the label of Darwin designer Tatiana Hoffmann. Her custom pieces are made to measure, so they fit the person who wears them perfectly.

Her sense of detail comes from home: her mother in Ukraine made winter hats, and her aunt worked in embroidery. That precision and care runs through each piece.
