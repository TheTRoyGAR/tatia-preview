What this website collects

This website does not collect your name, email or any other details itself. It has no sign-up, no online form and no shop yet. It does not use advertising cookies or analytics.

When you message us on Instagram

If you tap a button to message TATIA™ on Instagram, you leave this website and use Instagram. Instagram (Meta) handles that conversation under its own privacy policy. TATIA™ uses what you write only to answer you about your order or question.

Fonts

The text on this site uses fonts loaded from Google Fonts. Because of that, your browser contacts Google when a page loads, and Google may see your IP address.

When we add an online order form

We will update this page before it goes live, to say what we ask for, why, and how long we keep it. We will never sell your details.
